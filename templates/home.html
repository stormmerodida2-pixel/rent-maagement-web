Features

Everything between "rent is due" and "rent is in the bank"

One place for your properties, tenants, payments and repairs, whether you run one block or ten.

{% icon 'phone' %}

M-Pesa STK Push

Send a payment prompt straight to a tenant's phone. They enter their PIN and the payment lands on their lease.

{% icon 'inbox' %}

Automatic Paybill matching

Tenants pay your Paybill with their account number and every payment is matched to the right lease. Nothing to reconcile.

{% icon 'chart' %}

Arrears at a glance

Monthly rent is billed automatically. See who owes what, how much you've collected and your occupancy in one dashboard.

{% icon 'bell' %}

SMS reminders & receipts

Polite, automatic reminders to tenants with a balance, and an SMS receipt the moment their payment arrives.

{% icon 'wrench' %}

Maintenance tracking

Tenants report problems from their phones. Track priority, who's fixing it, the cost and when it was resolved.

{% icon 'shield' %}

Your data stays yours

Each landlord sees only their own properties, tenants and payments. Every M-Pesa receipt is recorded exactly once.

How it works

Set up once, then let rent come in

Add your properties and tenants

Create your buildings and units, add tenants with their M-Pesa numbers, and start a lease with the rent and due date.

Rent is billed every month

Each lease is charged on the 1st. Tenants get an SMS with their balance, your Paybill number and their account number.

Payments reconcile themselves

STK and Paybill payments are recorded the moment Safaricom confirms them. Balances, arrears and receipts update instantly.

For tenants

A private page for every tenant, no app or password needed

Each tenant gets a personal link by SMS. From any phone they can:

  • See their balance and payment history
  • Pay with one tap. The M-Pesa prompt comes to their own phone.
  • Report a leaking tap or broken lock, and follow the repair
