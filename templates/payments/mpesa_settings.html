Where to get these

  1. Sign in at developer.safaricom.co.ke and create an app with Lipa Na M-Pesa enabled. Its consumer key and secret are on the app page.
  2. Sandbox: use short code 174379 and the passkey shown under the passkey field.
  3. Live: use Go Live on the Daraja portal to link your own Paybill/Till. Safaricom emails the production passkey to the business admin.
