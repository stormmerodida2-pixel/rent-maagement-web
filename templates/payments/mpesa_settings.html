How to get your Daraja keys

  1. Create a Daraja account. Sign up at developer.safaricom.co.ke and log in.
  2. Create an app. Under My Apps, add an app and tick M-Pesa Express (for prompts) and C2B (for Paybill/Till payments). The app page shows its Consumer Key and Consumer Secret.
  3. Optional: try it in Sandbox. Choose Sandbox above, short code 174379, the sandbox passkey shown under the passkey field, and your sandbox app's keys. Save, then Test.
  4. Go live. On the Daraja portal choose Go Live, pick your Paybill or Till and confirm with the code Safaricom sends to the business administrator's phone. This needs the business administrator set up on the M-Pesa business portal; Safaricom can help with that.
  5. Use the live keys here. Safaricom emails the live passkey. Choose Live above, paste the live app's keys and passkey{% if account.is_till %} and your Head Office / Store number{% endif %}, save, then Test and Register.

Stuck? Contact us and we'll go through it with you. In the meantime, statement uploads keep your records up to date.
