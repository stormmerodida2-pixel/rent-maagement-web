Setting up (once)

  1. A number for {{ APP_NAME }}. Use a phone number that isn't already on WhatsApp (a new SIM is easiest). It becomes {{ APP_NAME }}'s WhatsApp Business number.
  2. Meta Business account. At business.facebook.com, create one for your business and verify it (Meta asks for business documents).
  3. WhatsApp app. At developers.facebook.com, create an app, add WhatsApp, and add your number under API Setup. Copy the Phone number ID shown there.
  4. Permanent token. In Business settings → System users, add a system user, give it the WhatsApp app with whatsapp_business_messaging permission, and generate a token that doesn't expire. Paste it above.
  5. Message template. In WhatsApp Manager → Message templates, create a Utility template named payments_summary (language English), with this body exactly:
    Meta usually approves it within minutes to a day.
  6. Test. Save the details above and send yourself a test.

Meta charges for messages a business starts (see WhatsApp pricing for current rates in Kenya). At most one per landlord per day, and only on days with payments, keeps it low.
