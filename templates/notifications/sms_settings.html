Where to find these

  1. Log in at account.africastalking.com and open your app (not the sandbox).
  2. Username is shown at the top of the app's dashboard.
  3. API key: the app's Settings → API Key → Generate. Paste it here straight away; it's stored encrypted and never shown again.
  4. Sender ID (optional): request one under SMS → Sender IDs. Until it's approved, messages come from Africa's Talking's shared number.
  5. Tick Send real SMS, save, then Check connection (shows your balance, sends nothing) and Send test SMS.

If the check says the authentication is invalid, the account may not be activated for live messages yet: Africa's Talking support can confirm.
